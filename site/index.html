Proven before it ships.

An agent.py doesn't run in Copilot Studio. brainfreeze studio translates what it can into agent flows and connector code, then runs every translation beside your agent's real Python on every test case, and refuses any that differ. What it can't prove stays a skill, and it tells you why.

More: how it's checked
  • Before deploying: each translation runs beside the agent's real Python on every test case. The build prints a parity line per agent, such as InvoiceRouter 72/72 PROVEN, and refuses a translation with a single difference.
  • While deploying: every component is read back from your environment and checked against what was built.
  • After deploying: it's a Draft until you ask your AI to publish it. Its test chat shows the real answers.
  • As you: the deploy uses your own delegated sign-in. It refuses app-only tokens and needs no service account.
